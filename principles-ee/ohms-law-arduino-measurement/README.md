# Ohm's Law & Arduino Measurement

## Overview

Explored the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance through breadboard experiments and electrical measurements. The work included measuring resistor values and observing how current changed as the applied source voltage was varied.

## Engineering Concepts

- Ohm's law
- Voltage and current measurement
- Resistance measurement
- Breadboard circuit construction
- Digital multimeter usage
- Arduino-based measurement/instrumentation

## Experimental Work

A resistive circuit was constructed and tested while the source voltage was varied across multiple values. Current measurements were recorded to compare experimental behavior with the expected voltage-current relationship.

**Context:** Principles of Electrical Engineering I laboratory work.
