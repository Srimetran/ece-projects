# Resistive Networks & Voltage Division

## Overview

Analyzed and constructed resistor networks to study equivalent resistance, voltage division, and proportionality. Circuit calculations were compared with physical measurements and simulation.

## Engineering Concepts

- Series and parallel resistance
- Equivalent resistance
- Voltage-divider relationships
- Proportionality in linear resistive circuits
- Circuit measurement
- LTspice simulation

## Key Takeaways

The project connected analytical circuit calculations to real component measurements, showing how resistor-network structure determines node voltages and equivalent resistance.

**Context:** Principles of Electrical Engineering I laboratory work.
